▾Layers

The overlay is a group of four layers above the source: Ink bleed and Ink pooling (Multiply), Ink gaps (Normal: shows the paper under your layer through the ink) and Paper (Multiply). Light ink on dark paper swaps Multiply and Screen. Hide Ink gaps, or lower Dry skip, Mottling and Pooling, for solid ink. Change any layer's opacity, or hide it, to fine-tune.
