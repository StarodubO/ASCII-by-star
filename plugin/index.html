▾Layers

The overlay is a group of four layers above the source: Ink bleed and Ink pooling (Multiply), Ink gaps (Screen) and Paper (Multiply). Light ink on dark paper swaps Multiply and Screen. Change any layer's opacity, or hide it, to fine-tune.
