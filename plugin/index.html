Hide a message in the picture: put it in curly braces, e.g. {HELLO}. {XO*5} hides it 5 times.

Fill the artwork: [STAR] writes STAR all over it, [STAR|LOVE|★] takes turns, [?STAR|LOVE|★] picks at random, [?01] random characters. Characters outside the brackets (.:-= [STAR]) draw the lighter tones.

These characters stay. {HELLO} hides a message, {XO*5} 5 times. [STAR] writes STAR over the darker tones, [STAR|LOVE] takes turns, [?STAR|LOVE] picks at random. Other characters are ignored.

Your texture is the paper layer and shapes the bleed: ink skips its raised parts and pools in its dips. Dark scans (noise, dust) are inverted on light paper. The fibre settings below still steer feathering and wicking.

▾Layers

The overlay is a group of four layers above the source: Ink bleed and Ink pooling (Multiply), Ink gaps (Normal: shows the paper under your layer through the ink) and Paper (Multiply). Light ink on dark paper swaps Multiply and Screen. Hide Ink gaps, or lower Dry skip, Mottling and Pooling, for solid ink. Change any layer's opacity, or hide it, to fine-tune.

▾Look
Lines

Contour lines join points of equal height, like a map. Ridge lines are rows lifted by the height, with nearer rows hiding the ones behind. A look (or Randomize) sets the lines and colours and keeps the height settings.
