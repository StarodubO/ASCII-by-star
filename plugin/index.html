▾Layers

The overlay is a group of four layers above the source: Ink bleed and Ink pooling (Multiply), Ink gaps (Normal: shows the paper under your layer through the ink) and Paper (Multiply). Light ink on dark paper swaps Multiply and Screen. Hide Ink gaps, or lower Dry skip, Mottling and Pooling, for solid ink. Change any layer's opacity, or hide it, to fine-tune.

▾Look
Lines

Contour lines join points of equal height, like a map. Ridge lines are rows lifted by the height, with nearer rows hiding the ones behind. Labyrinth grows winding lines, close together where the height is low (dark) and wide apart where it's high. Point cloud scatters points and lifts them towards you by the height (a depth map works best), seen from a turned camera. A look (or Randomize) sets the lines and colours and keeps the height settings.
